About Film Collection

Film Collection is a learning project built with Angular. It demonstrates routing, standalone components, custom directives, custom pipes, and Angular Signals for state handling.

What You Can Do

  • Browse all films on the Home page.
  • Filter films by title in real time.
  • Mark films as favorites.
  • Open a film details page with full information.

Tech Stack

  • Angular (standalone API)
  • TypeScript (strict mode)
  • Signals for reactive state
